Trust & security

Each school's data stays its own.

PHSMS serves many schools from one platform, and every record is tied to its school. One school can never see another's data.

Access checked on every record

Roles decide what you can do. A second check decides whether you may touch this particular student, invoice or grade.

An audit trail you can read

Every change records who did it and when, and the school's audit log is there for owners and auditors to review.

Sensitive records, extra care

Health, guidance and safeguarding records are kept apart, left out of exports and dashboards, and every access is logged.

Safe sign-in

Secure cookie sessions, hashed passwords and rate-limited sign-in.

Controlled exports

Reports and data exports are permission-checked, logged, and delivered by expiring links.

Two-person controls

Payroll runs and large discounts need approval from someone other than the person who prepared them.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is it only for one school?

No. PHSMS is multi-tenant: many schools run on one platform, each with its own data, logo and brand colour.

Can we bring our existing student records?

Yes. Upload CSV or Excel files for students, guardians, enrolments, staff, subjects, sections and fees. Every row is validated, and a whole import can be rolled back.

Can parents pay online?

Not yet. Today the cashier records payments (cash, bank transfer, cheque or e-wallet) and parents see their statements and receipts in the portal. Online payment is planned for a later release.

When do parents see grades?

Only after the grades are published. Grades move from draft to submitted to approved to published, and parents never see drafts.

Does it support campuses and senior high strands?

Yes. A school can have several campuses, and senior high strands are part of the academic structure alongside grade levels from Kinder to Grade 12.

How are announcements delivered?

In the app and by email. SMS is not available yet.

Who can see health and guidance records?

These records are kept separate from the student file, excluded from exports and dashboards, and every access is logged for review.
